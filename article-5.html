New website feature - Radio Tokyo News!

Keeping up with Japan’s fast-moving pop-culture circuit usually means wrestling with fragmented social feeds, auto-translated leaks, and an endless barrage of clickbait. Starting today, we’re cutting through that noise.

Welcome to Radio Tokyo News our brand-new editorial and audio wire delivering curated, no-nonsense dispatches straight from the heart of Akihabara, Shibuya, and beyond.

What’s the Signal About?

Think of it as part pirate radio, part focused industry desk. Built directly into our site’s dashboard, this new feature gives you sharp, concise coverage across four core lanes:

  • Studio Radar: Behind-the-scenes production updates, director moves, and animator (sakuga) highlights.
  • Akiba Underground: Niche gaming scoops, indie dojin releases, and Japan's evolving arcade landscape.
  • The Manga Wire: Early serialization impressions, industry shifts, and translated creator commentary.
  • Tokyo Ambience: Curated lofi audio and field recordings from Tokyo's alleyways, retro game centers, and midnight transit lines.

How to Tune In

We kept the design lightweight and non-intrusive so you can keep reading, browsing, or gaming without disruption:

The Mini-Player: Tap the vintage terminal icon in the top-right corner of your screen to stream our quick audio roundups alongside relaxing city-pop and synth beats.

The dial is set, the first transmissions are already live on the homepage, and the stream is running.

Plug in your headphones, check out the widget, and let us know in the comments: what niche topics do you want us to broadcast next?
